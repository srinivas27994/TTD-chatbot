About this assistant

TTD Seva Assistant answers questions about darshan, sevas, accommodation, timings, transport, prasadam and donations at Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams.

Answers come from an AI model, so ticket availability, prices and timings can change. Confirm anything time-sensitive on tirumala.org or the official booking portal ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in.

Never share your Aadhaar number, card details or OTP in this chat.
